2. Story: Home Economics Class

Read the panels and click the audio button to hear the narration.

1

The children are at school in the home economics class.

Mrs. Clark: Today I'm going to tell you about table manners. Please, sit down at the table. Donna, don't put your elbows on the table.

Donna: OK, Mrs. Clark.

2

Donna: Fay, pass me the salt, please.

Tina: I need it, too! Give it to me!

Mrs. Clark: Tina! Be polite and don't talk with your mouth full. Susan, don't put the napkin around your neck. Put it on your lap.

Susan: Sorry, Mrs. Clark.

3

The next day...

Mrs. Clark: Let's see what you remember. Look at this quiz.

Donna: Hmm... I don't remember number 3.

Susan: I remember it well!

Fay: Of course you do! Ha ha ha!

4

On Saturday evening at home.

Susan: Ann, don't put your elbows on the table.

Ann: You're right, Susan! Well done!

Susan: I can tell you more about table manners later.
